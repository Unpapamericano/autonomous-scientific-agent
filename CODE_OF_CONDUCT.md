# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include
empathy, constructive feedback, and respect for different viewpoints.
Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination,
sexualized language or imagery, and publishing private information without
permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers through
the contact method listed in the project profile. Maintainers will review
reports fairly and may remove or reject contributions that violate this code.

This code is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
